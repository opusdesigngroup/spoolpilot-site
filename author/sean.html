SpoolPilot

Sean

Sean runs Opus Design Company, a 3D-print shop on Etsy with more than 3,000 sales, and built SpoolPilot to see what each of those orders actually made. Every guide on this site is what that shop tracks and why.

The shop

Opus Design Company sells 3D-printed coffee gear on Etsy: dosing stands for grinders, filter holders and brewer accessories, printed on a few printers in a spare room. As of October 2026 the shop shows 3,100+ sales and a 4.8-star rating from more than 700 reviews.

The guides on this site come out of running that shop: the fees Opus Design Company pays on each order, the way it prices a new listing, and the way it keeps count of sealed spools.

Why SpoolPilot exists

In Sean’s words: “I could never get a straight answer on what an order actually made after Etsy’s fees, filament, and my time. Spreadsheets fell behind, and every other tool wanted a desktop. So I built the one I wanted: real profit, in my pocket, right at the printer.”

SpoolPilot is an iPhone app published by Opus Design Group LLC. It reads the fees Etsy charged on each order, subtracts filament, electricity and packaging, and shows the profit per sale, per product and per month.
