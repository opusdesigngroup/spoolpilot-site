SpoolPilot

I’m Sean. I sell 3D prints on Etsy, and I built SpoolPilot.

I could never get a straight answer on what an order actually made after Etsy’s fees, filament, and my time. Spreadsheets fell behind, and every other tool wanted a desktop. So I built the one I wanted: real profit, in my pocket, right at the printer.
