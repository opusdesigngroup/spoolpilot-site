Shopify

Your store is connected.

SpoolPilot runs on your iPhone — that's where your numbers live, and where your shop data stays. One more step and your Shopify orders start reporting real profit.

Get the app

Enter your email and we'll send you the link to get started.

No spam, and a person reads every reply.

Then, in the app

Open Settings → Sales channels → Shopify and tap Connect. You've already approved SpoolPilot on Shopify's side, so this takes a couple of seconds — it's just handing the connection to your phone.

From there SpoolPilot imports your products and orders, and reads the real processing fee Shopify charged on each one.

What SpoolPilot reads

Order line items, quantities, prices, discounts, shipping charged, currency and order dates — the figures that decide what a product actually earned.

Not your customers. SpoolPilot asks Shopify for no names, email addresses, phone numbers, or shipping and billing addresses. None of it is requested, received, or stored. Your shop data lives on your device, not on our servers — the details are in the privacy policy.

Changed your mind?

Uninstall SpoolPilot from your Shopify admin under Settings → Apps and sales channels. That revokes its access immediately; nothing of yours is kept.
