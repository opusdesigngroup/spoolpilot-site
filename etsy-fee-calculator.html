The honest caveat: this is an estimate

Real orders drift from rate-card math. Coupons change the base the percentages apply to. Offsite Ads hit only attributed orders — at 12% or 15% depending on your revenue, capped per order. Processing rates differ by country. Regulatory fees appear in some regions and not others. Stack a few of those and the number above can be off by enough to matter on a $28 sale.

That's why SpoolPilot doesn't estimate. It connects to your Etsy shop and reads the actual fees Etsy charged on each order — itemized line by line, built to reconcile with what Etsy deposits. Estimates for planning; actuals for the truth.
